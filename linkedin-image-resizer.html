How to use LinkedIn image resizer

  1. Choose a profile photo, personal banner, company banner or post image.
  2. Add your image and position it.
  3. Watch the guide that shows where your profile photo covers the banner.
  4. Save and upload the result.

Why use this tool

LinkedIn is where people form a quick first impression, and a well-sized photo and banner look professional. Your profile photo covers part of the banner, so a good resizer shows you which area stays hidden and keeps the important part in view.

Tips for best results

  • Use a clear, friendly headshot with a plain background.
  • Keep banner text clear of the profile photo area.
  • Use a wide banner image so it looks sharp on large screens.

Frequently asked questions

What size is the LinkedIn banner?

A personal banner is commonly 1584 by 396 pixels. Sizes can change, so check LinkedIn's current guidance.

What is a good profile photo size?

A square photo of at least 400 by 400 pixels is a common choice.

Is it free and private?

Yes. The tool runs in your browser and does not upload your picture.
