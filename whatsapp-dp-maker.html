How to use WhatsApp DP maker

  1. Add the photo you want to use.
  2. See the circle preview, or choose to show the whole photo with no cropping.
  3. Move and zoom until it looks right.
  4. Save the picture and set it as your WhatsApp photo.

Why use this tool

WhatsApp crops profile pictures into a circle, which often cuts off heads or the edges of a picture. This tool shows you the circle in advance, or fits the whole photo onto a square canvas, so nothing important is lost.

Tips for best results

  • Keep the important part of the picture near the centre.
  • If the whole picture matters, use the no-crop option.
  • Use a sharp photo. WhatsApp shows it in a large view when someone taps it.

Frequently asked questions

What size is a WhatsApp profile picture?

WhatsApp uses a square image shown inside a circle. A square picture of at least 500 pixels works well.

Can I keep the full photo?

Yes. Choose the option that fits the whole photo with no cropping.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. It stays on your device.
