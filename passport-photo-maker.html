How to use Passport photo maker

  1. Choose your country or enter a custom size.
  2. Add a photo of yourself and move it until your face lines up with the guide.
  3. Save the photo at the exact size.
  4. Optionally print several copies on one sheet.

Why use this tool

Passport and visa photos must follow strict rules about size, head position and background. Making your own at home saves money and a trip to a studio, as long as you follow your country's official rules. The guide overlay helps you place your face the way it should be.

Tips for best results

  • Use a plain, light background, even lighting and a neutral expression.
  • Take the photo from eye level, straight on, without filters.
  • Always confirm the requirements on your government's official site before you submit.

Frequently asked questions

Will my photo be accepted?

Acceptance is decided by the issuing authority. Follow their rules about background, lighting, expression and size.

What size do I need?

It depends on the country, for example 2 by 2 inches in the United States and 35 by 45 millimetres in many other countries. Check the official page.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. It is edited entirely in your browser.
