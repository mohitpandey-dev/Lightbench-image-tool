How to use Meme generator

  1. Add the picture you want to turn into a meme.
  2. Type your top and bottom text. Add an extra line if you need a third.
  3. Drag any line of text on the picture to move it, and adjust the size and outline.
  4. Make the meme, then download it or copy it straight into a chat.

Why use this tool

Most meme sites add a watermark, ask you to sign up or upload your picture to a server. This one does none of that. Your picture stays on your device, the text is drawn on it right in your browser, and the download is yours with nothing added.

Tips for best results

  • Keep each line short. Memes read best at a glance, and short lines can be bigger.
  • White text with a thick black outline stays readable on almost any picture.
  • If a line covers a face, drag it up or down, or make the text a little smaller.
  • Save as PNG if the text looks soft. Save as JPG for a smaller file to share.

Frequently asked questions

Can I move the text?

Yes. Drag a line of text on the preview. You can also select a line and use the arrow keys to nudge it. Hold Shift for bigger steps.

Why does the text look different on another device?

The classic Impact font comes with Windows and Mac. On other devices a similar bold font is used instead. The meme you download keeps the look you see in the preview.

Is my picture uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser and your picture never leaves your device.

Does it add a watermark?

No. The download has only your picture and your text.
