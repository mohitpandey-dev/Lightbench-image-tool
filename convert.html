Convert image format

Change an image between JPG, PNG and WebP.

Transparent areas become white in JPG. PNG keeps transparency.

Download image

How to use Convert image format

  1. Choose or drop the image you want to change.
  2. Pick the format you need: JPG, PNG or WebP.
  3. Press convert and check the preview.
  4. Download the new file.

Why use this tool

Different places want different formats. A form may only accept JPG, a logo may need the transparent background that PNG keeps, and a website may prefer WebP because the files are smaller. Converting lets you meet those requirements without installing any software.

Tips for best results

  • Convert to JPG for photos and to PNG for logos, screenshots and anything with transparency.
  • Converting a JPG to PNG does not bring back lost quality. It only changes the container.
  • Transparent areas turn into a solid colour when you save as JPG, because JPG cannot store transparency.

Frequently asked questions

Which format should I choose?

JPG for photos, PNG for graphics and screenshots, WebP when you want smaller files and the website supports it. Our format guide explains the trade-offs.

Will converting make the picture worse?

Converting to JPG or lossy WebP applies some compression, so a tiny loss is possible. PNG is lossless.

Can I convert many images at once?

Convert images one after another. Each file is processed on your device, so it stays private.
