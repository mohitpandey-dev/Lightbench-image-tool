About Us

Welcome to Lightbench, your all-in-one workstation for fast, secure, and entirely client-side web utilities.

We believe that everyday digital tasks—like resizing social media assets, extracting text via OCR, removing image metadata, or converting document formats—shouldn't require downloading bulky software or uploading your private files to external servers. That is why we built Lightbench.

Our Philosophy: Local, Secure, and Instant

Unlike traditional online conversion websites, Lightbench processes your files directly within your web browser. Your images, PDFs, and text snippets never leave your device, ensuring total privacy and near-instant processing speeds. We bridge the gap between high-powered utility desktop software and the convenience of a modern web application.

Whether you are a developer optimizing design assets, a professional handling sensitive PDFs, or someone quickly resizing a profile picture, Lightbench provides clean, ad-free, and hyper-focused tools to get the job done efficiently.
