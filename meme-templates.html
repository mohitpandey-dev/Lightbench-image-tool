How to use Meme templates

  1. Pick a template from the list. Its layout opens in the editor.
  2. Type your text in the boxes. Each one is placed in its own panel.
  3. Drag a line if you want it somewhere else, and change the size or colour.
  4. Make the meme and download it.

Why use this tool

Templates save you from the blank start. Each one here is a simple layout made for this site, so you can use it freely: a split for before and after, a no and yes pair, three panels, and plain backgrounds. Because nothing is uploaded, you can try as many as you like.

Tips for best results

  • Use "Before and after" for comparisons, and "No and yes" for choices.
  • The plain white template turns the tool into a text post maker.
  • Short text fits panels best. Lower the text size if a line gets crowded.
  • You can still use your own picture. Drop it in the box under the templates.

Frequently asked questions

Are these famous meme pictures?

No. These are original layouts, so you do not need to worry about someone else's picture. Use your own photo if you want a specific image.

Can I change the text colour?

Yes. Use the Style section below the preview.

Is my picture uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser and your picture never leaves your device.
