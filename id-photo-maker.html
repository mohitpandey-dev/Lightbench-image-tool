How to use ID photo maker

  1. Choose a size or type your own width and height.
  2. Add a photo and line the face up with the guide.
  3. Save the cropped photo.
  4. Print several copies on one sheet if you need them.

Why use this tool

School cards, job badges, library cards and application forms all ask for a small portrait in a particular size. This tool crops your picture to that size and lets you print multiple copies on a single sheet, so you can cut them out at home.

Tips for best results

  • Check the exact size the form asks for, in millimetres or pixels.
  • Use even lighting without shadows across the face.
  • When you print, choose actual size so the copies are not scaled.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use any size?

Yes. Pick a preset or enter your own width and height.

How do I print copies?

Choose the printable sheet option, then print at 100 percent scale on photo paper.

Is my photo kept?

No. Everything happens in your browser and nothing is saved.
