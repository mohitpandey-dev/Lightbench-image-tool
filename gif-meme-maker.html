How to use GIF meme maker

  1. Add the animated GIF you want to caption.
  2. Type your top and bottom text and watch it appear in the live preview.
  3. Choose the width and speed of the new GIF.
  4. Make the GIF meme and download it.

Why use this tool

A caption turns an ordinary reaction GIF into your own joke. This tool reads every frame of the GIF, draws your text on all of them, and builds a new GIF. The work is done in your browser, so the GIF is never uploaded.

Tips for best results

  • Smaller widths make much smaller files. 320 px is fine for chats.
  • Short text is easier to read in a moving picture.
  • Use a thick outline when the GIF has bright or busy scenes.
  • Very long GIFs lose some frames to keep the file a sensible size.

Frequently asked questions

Why can it not open my GIF?

It needs a browser that can read the frames of a GIF. The latest Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox can. Also check that the file is a real GIF, not a video.

Will the new GIF be bigger than the original?

It can be. Choose a smaller width or a faster speed to bring the file size down.

Can I turn a video into a GIF meme?

Not here. This tool starts from a GIF.

Is my picture uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser and your picture never leaves your device.
