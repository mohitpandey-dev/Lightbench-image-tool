Handwriting to text

Try to read a handwritten note into text you can edit. Handwriting is much harder to read automatically than print, so check the result carefully.

The first time you recognise text, your browser downloads a small recognition engine and language file, so it needs an internet connection. After that it is cached for next time.

This works best on clear, printed (non-cursive) handwriting, dark ink on plain paper, and one paragraph at a time.

How to use Handwriting to text

  1. Add a clear photo of the handwritten note.
  2. Run the reader and wait for the result.
  3. Correct the words it got wrong.
  4. Copy the text to wherever you need it.

Why use this tool

Handwriting is much harder for a computer to read than print, because every person forms letters differently. This tool gives it a try, which is often enough to get most of a note into editable form, but you should expect to fix some words.

Tips for best results

  • Neat block letters in dark ink on plain paper give the best results.
  • Photograph the page in daylight, flat and straight on.
  • Treat the output as a first draft and read it against the original.

Frequently asked questions

Why is handwriting so inaccurate?

Joined-up writing, slants and personal styles vary far more than printed fonts.

Will it work on my cursive?

Cursive is the hardest case. Expect many mistakes.

Is my note uploaded?

No. It is read in your browser and stays on your device.
