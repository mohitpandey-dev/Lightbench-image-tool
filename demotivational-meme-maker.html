How to use Demotivational meme maker

  1. Add a picture. Landscape photos work best.
  2. Type a short title in capitals and a small line under it.
  3. Choose the font and the title size.
  4. Make the poster, then download it.

Why use this tool

The demotivational poster is a joke on motivational posters: a serious frame, a one-word title and a dry line underneath. The layout is fixed, so you only need the picture and the words.

Tips for best results

  • One word or a short phrase works best for the title.
  • Make the small line the punchline, and keep it to one or two lines.
  • Use a serif font for the classic look.
  • Pick a photo that is calm and well lit. The black frame makes it stand out.

Frequently asked questions

Can I change the border?

The white border and black frame are part of the style, so they stay fixed.

Why is the title in capitals?

It is part of the classic look. The small line keeps the letters you type.

Is my picture uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser and your picture never leaves your device.
