Contact sheet maker

Print a page of thumbnails, with file names and page numbers. Good for reviewing a shoot or sharing a set of photos.

    Download Open in a new tab

    How to use Contact sheet maker

    1. Add all the photos you want to review.
    2. Choose how many thumbnails fit on each page.
    3. Turn file names and page numbers on or off.
    4. Create the sheet and print or save it.

    Why use this tool

    A contact sheet shows many small thumbnails on one page, the way photographers have reviewed a roll of film for decades. It is ideal for picking the best shots from a shoot, sharing a whole set with a client, or keeping a printable index of a folder.

    Tips for best results

    • Include file names so people can tell you exactly which picture they want.
    • Use a larger thumbnail size when you need to judge sharpness.
    • Print on A4 or Letter paper at actual size for the best layout.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is a contact sheet used for?

    It is used to review and choose from many photos at a glance, and to keep a printed index of a set.

    Can I save it as a PDF?

    You can print the page and choose Save as PDF in your browser print dialog.

    Are my photos uploaded?

    No. The sheet is built on your device.
