Image collage maker

Arrange several photos on one picture. Use an even grid, or rows where every photo keeps its own shape.

    Background
    Download collage

    How to use Image collage maker

    1. Add the photos you want to combine.
    2. Choose an even grid, or rows where every photo keeps its own shape.
    3. Adjust the spacing and arrangement until it looks right.
    4. Save the finished collage as one image.

    Why use this tool

    A collage puts several pictures into a single image, which is perfect for a before and after, a trip summary or a product comparison. One combined picture is also easier to post, because many platforms limit how many images you can attach.

    Tips for best results

    • Use photos with similar brightness so the collage looks balanced.
    • Rows that keep each photo's shape avoid awkward cropping of faces.
    • Keep the number of pictures small enough that each is still easy to see.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many photos can I add?

    You can add as many as your device handles comfortably. A handful gives the clearest result.

    Will my photos be cropped?

    The even grid crops to fit. The rows layout keeps each photo's own shape.

    Is anything uploaded?

    No. The collage is built in your browser.
