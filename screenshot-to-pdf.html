Screenshot to PDF

Paste a screenshot with Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on a Mac), or drop your files. Long screenshots can be split across pages.

    Download PDF Open in a new tab

    How to use Screenshot to PDF

    1. Take a screenshot, then press Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on a Mac) on the page, or drop the files in.
    2. Add more screenshots in the order you want.
    3. Choose how long screenshots should be split across pages.
    4. Create the PDF and download it.

    Why use this tool

    Screenshots are the quickest way to capture a receipt, a chat, a web page or an error. A PDF turns them into a document you can archive, print or send as proof. Long screenshots are split across pages instead of being shrunk until they are unreadable.

    Tips for best results

    • Capture at the highest resolution your device offers so text stays sharp.
    • Crop out notification bars and other clutter before you build the PDF.
    • Hide personal information in the screenshot before you share the PDF.

    Frequently asked questions

    Why split long screenshots?

    A very tall image would be shrunk to a thin strip on one page. Splitting keeps the text readable.

    Can I paste from my phone?

    On a phone, use the choose-file button. Pasting works best on a computer keyboard.

    Is it private?

    Yes. Your screenshots stay in your browser.
